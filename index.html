About the Data

Verdicts: GO / CAUTION / OFF WATER use the limits selected above (Seniors or Novices). Day verdicts use each day's strongest forecast wind and gusts, which usually come in the afternoon.

Lightning / CAPE: CAPE above 1000 J/kg = clear the water. The Lightning section shows live strikes (Blitzortung.org) or rain radar for the last 2 hours (RainViewer).

Tides: Forecast sea level off Fremantle (for the Swan and Canning River venues), Bunbury, Albany or Long Beach from Open-Meteo's ocean model (tide plus weather effects), shown relative to mean sea level. It is a model forecast for the open coast, not the official tide table: up a river or inside an inlet the tide runs later and its height can differ. Tide times are in the venue's own time. Check official tide tables for anything critical. All other venues are freshwater.

Wind: Forecast from Open-Meteo. Live observations from the nearest BOM station (named above) at the Australian venues; the status banner uses whichever of forecast or live wind is worse. At the international venues the live reading is the nearest airport's routine weather report, when one can be fetched; otherwise they are forecast only. Their course lines are provisional.

Days 17–21: A long-range outlook from the average of NOAA's GFS ensemble forecast (about a 50 km grid), via Open-Meteo. It is much less certain than the first 16 days and shows wind, rain and temperature only — use it for the trend, not for a decision.

Daylight: First and last light are civil twilight (sun 6° below the horizon), calculated in the page.

Data: Open-Meteo · Auto-refreshes every 10 min while open.

Days 8–21. Confidence drops the further out you look — treat the back end as a trend, not a precise forecast. Days 17–21 come from a coarser long-range forecast and show fewer readings. ★ marks the best days to row in days 8–16.

Getting around Weatherow





    

  1. Tap any of the six cards (Wind, Gusts, Temperature…) to open a graph of the next 6 to 24 hours. Tap it again to close.
    2. 

  2. Tap a section heading to fold it away or open it. The row of shortcuts under the cards jumps straight to a section.
    3. 

  3. Change venue with the button at the bottom: tap it for the full list, or swipe it left or right for the next venue. Star your regular venues to keep them at the top.
    4. 

  4. Pull down at the top of the page to refresh, or tap the refresh icon in the header.
    5. 

  5. ⚙ Settings holds your session times, regatta dates, section order, text size and units.
    6. 















Settings


Saved on this phone only.







Session times





















Add each regular session with its days. Weatherow shows a summary card for whichever comes next, at the selected venue. Switch a session Off to pause it without deleting it; with every session off, the card is hidden.






Regatta dates
















Add a regatta or race day for the venue you are on. A countdown card then shows at that venue, and from 21 days out it carries that day's forecast.






Sections


Use the arrows to change the order on the page. Hide the ones you don't use.










Layout








Compact fits the six readings in two rows of three and tightens the spacing, for checking several venues quickly. Tap a card to see its detail.






Text size









Makes the page's text bigger. Labels drawn inside the graphs and maps stay the same size.






Units















In knots or mph the wind limits are rounded to whole numbers, for example 20 km/h becomes 11 kn or 12 mph. Rain stays in millimetres.























Install Weatherow


Adds Weatherow to your home screen. It then opens full screen, like an app, with no browser bars.
